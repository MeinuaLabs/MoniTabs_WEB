MoniTabs vs Splitwise vs IOU Apps: Which Debt Tracker Fits You?

November 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: Splitwise for groups and shared bills. A simple IOU app if you only track money you lent. MoniTabs if you want your debts and your own monthly budget in one free Android app.

We make MoniTabs, so read this with that in mind. To keep it fair, we checked every other app's Google Play page and official website on 19 November 2026 and only list what they state there. Apps change, so check the current version before you decide.

Pick Splitwise if you split costs with a group

Splitwise is built for groups: trips, roommates and shared bills, with friends on Android, iPhone and the web. Everyone needs an account, because expenses are backed up online so the whole group sees the same numbers. The free version limits how many expenses you can add per day; Splitwise Pro (a subscription) removes that limit and the ads, and adds currency conversion and charts. Splitwise itself says it “isn't a full personal finance tool”.

Pick a simple IOU app if you only track lending

Who Owes Me and Debt Tracker: IOU & Money Owed focus on one job: who owes you and what you owe. Both need no account. Their reminders are stronger than MoniTabs' — Who Owes Me lets you snooze a reminder for an hour, a day or a week, and Debt Tracker reminds you before each payment. Debt Manager and Tracker has many installs, but its Play page shows its last update in August 2022, and CSV export needs the paid Pro version.

Pick MoniTabs if you want debts and your budget in one place

MoniTabs has three tabs: Wallet for your monthly income and spending (Needs, Wants, and Debts & Savings, with your own labels and charts — it fits the 50/30/20 rule), plus They Owe and I Owe for debts with partial payments and remaining balances. It exports PDF and Excel, supports multiple currencies, and needs no account; your entries are stored on your phone. It is free with ads, and a short video removes them for six hours.

Where MoniTabs is not the best choice

  • No groups or sharing. For a trip with friends or a shared flat, use Splitwise.
  • Android only. There is no iPhone or web version.
  • One-time debt reminders. If you want repeating or snoozable reminders, Who Owes Me or Debt Tracker do that better.
  • Everything is entered by hand. There is no bank connection.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Splitwise?

Yes. If you track money between friends on Android and do not need shared groups, apps like MoniTabs, Debt Tracker: IOU & Money Owed, and Who Owes Me are free with ads and need no account. For group trips and shared bills with friends on iPhone or the web, Splitwise is still the better fit.

What app tracks money I lent to friends?

Personal IOU trackers are made for this: you add a person, an amount and a due date, then log repayments. MoniTabs, Debt Tracker: IOU & Money Owed and Who Owes Me all do this on Android without an account. MoniTabs also tracks your own monthly income and spending in the same app.

Can I track debts without creating an account?

Yes. MoniTabs, Debt Tracker: IOU & Money Owed and Who Owes Me work without sign-up. Splitwise needs an account, because it backs expenses up online so everyone in a group can see them.
