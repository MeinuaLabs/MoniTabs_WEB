1. Introduction

MoniTabs ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Data you create and store

When you use MoniTabs, you voluntarily provide personal financial information including:

  • Debt information — names, amounts, currencies, dates, notes
  • Transactions — payment records, borrowing records
  • Wallet data — income / expense records, categories, labels
  • Custom labels and monthly notes
  • App settings and reminder preferences

Important: all this data is stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit, store, or access this data on remote servers.

2.2 Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions for functionality:

  • INTERNET — to load Google AdMob advertisements.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — required by Google Play Services for ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to send debt reminder notifications.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to schedule reminders at precise times.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to reschedule reminders after a device reboot.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (API ≤ 28) — to save backup files to device storage.

2.3 Automatically collected information

When you view ads, Google AdMob collects limited non-personalized ad data (impressions, clicks). This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How we use your information

  • Local usage. To display and manage your debts, calculate totals, and generate reminders.
  • Ad serving. To deliver and display Google AdMob advertisements.
  • Backup & export. To create .json backup files at your request.

4. Data security

  • Local storage. All data is stored in a local SQLite database (Room Database) on your device. We do not have access to this data.
  • No cloud backup. Your data is never automatically synced to our servers.
  • Device backup. Android's system-level backup (Google Cloud) may back up app data if enabled in your device settings.

5. Data retention & deletion

  • Retention. Data persists on your device until manually deleted.
  • Deletion. You can delete individual records or use the "Clear All Data" feature in settings.
  • Device removal. Uninstalling the App deletes all local data (unless synced via Android's system backup).

6. Third-party services

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK. Used to display ads. Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • File sharing. When you export backups, data is handled by the third-party app you choose (e.g., Gmail, Drive).

7. Your rights & choices

  • Data access. You have full access to edit, export, or delete your data within the App.
  • Rights for certain regions. Users in regions like the EU (GDPR) can exercise their rights to access or delete data through the App's built-in tools.

8. Children's privacy

MoniTabs is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. International users

By using the App, you consent to the transfer and processing of information in the Republic of Armenia, where the App was developed, and other countries where ad services operate.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes are effective when posted to the App.
