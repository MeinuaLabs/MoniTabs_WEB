FAQ

Common questions.

Is MoniTabs free?+

Yes. Free to download from Google Play. The app shows ads to support development — no subscriptions, no paywalls.

Can I remove the ads?+

Yes. Watch a short rewarded video and the app stays ad-free for the next 6 hours. Watch another when it expires — that's it.

Will my data sync across devices?+

No — by design. Use the in-app backup & restore feature to move data manually between devices.

What happens if I uninstall the app?+

The local database is removed with the app. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your data.

Can I use multiple currencies?+

Yes. Each transaction or debt has its own currency, and totals are grouped per-currency automatically.

Do reminders work without internet?+

Yes. Reminders use Android's local notification and alarm system — no internet needed.

Is there an iOS version?+

Android only for now.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?+

Email meinua.labs@gmail.com with details — repro steps for bugs, the use case for features.
