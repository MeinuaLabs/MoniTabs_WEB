1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nutix: Nut & Bolt Sort Puzzle ("the Game", "we") collects, why, and your choices. The Game is made by Meinua Labs. It does not require an account or sign-in, and we never ask for your name, e-mail or contacts.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, helpers, settings and the daily-reward streak) is stored locally on your device. If Android backup is turned on, this progress may be included in your Google account backup, which is controlled by Google and your device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob: banner ads on the map and at the bottom of the game screen, ads between levels, optional reward videos that you choose to watch, and an ad when you return to the Game after a break. AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information;
  • ad interaction data (impressions and clicks).

These are used to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, only where you consent, personalise ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Consent (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF v2) before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy choices. Your choices are stored on your device and also applied to Firebase through Google consent mode.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or completed, helpers used, reward video watched) and device and app information, used to improve game balance and design.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports (error details, device model, Android version) used to fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config: downloads game settings such as prices and reward amounts. It uses an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous) and Cloud Firestore: used only for the "Invite friends" feature. An anonymous ID and your invite code are stored so that a friend who opens the Game from your link can be credited to you.

Firebase is provided by Google LLC: Firebase privacy information.

6. Invite links (install referrer)

When you install the Game from a friend's invite link, the Google Play Install Referrer API tells the Game which invite code was used. You get a welcome pack, and the friend who invited you receives an in-game reward (the Donut nut skin). Nothing else about you is shared with them.

7. Notifications

Reminder notifications are off by default and are only sent if you turn them on. They are created on your device (no push server) and limited to one per day. You can turn them off in the Game's Settings or in Android settings.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Analytics data is kept according to Firebase settings (up to 14 months). To delete invite-related data, e-mail us your invite code (shown in the Game under Settings → Invite friends).

10. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
