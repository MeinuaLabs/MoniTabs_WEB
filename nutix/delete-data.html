Nutix: Nut & Bolt Sort Puzzle by Meinua Labs

This page explains how to delete the data collected by the game Nutix: Nut & Bolt Sort Puzzle, published on Google Play by Meinua Labs. The game has no user accounts: you never sign in, and we never ask for your name or e-mail.

How to request deletion

  1. Find your invite code (8 letters and numbers). It is the only way we can find your data, because the game has no accounts.
    • Version 1.0.2 and newer: open Nutix → Settings; the code is shown under Invite friends.
    • Older versions: tap Invite friends and share the message to yourself (for example to Notes or e-mail). The code is the last part of the link, after invite_code.
  2. Send an e-mail to meinua.labs@gmail.com with the subject "Nutix – delete my data" and your invite code in the message.
  3. We delete your data and confirm by e-mail within 30 days.

If you cannot open the game anymore, e-mail us anyway and describe the request; without the invite code we may not be able to identify your data.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player ID (Firebase Authentication) and your invite code.
  • Your invite records in our database (Cloud Firestore): who invited you and which friends you invited.
  • We also ask Google Firebase to delete the analytics and crash data linked to your app instance, where it can be identified.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Game progress, coins and settings are stored only on your phone, not on our servers. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Nutix → Storage → Clear data).
  • Analytics data (Firebase Analytics) that cannot be linked to you is deleted automatically after at most 14 months. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Ad data is processed by Google AdMob under Google's own privacy policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Purchases made through Google Play are kept by Google Play under its own terms.

More information

See the Nutix Privacy Policy and the Nutix Terms of Service. Questions: meinua.labs@gmail.com.
